# Research line A: frontier / foundational physics

🟢 established anchors, 🟡 synthesis, 🔴 speculative — labelled inline.

This line is deliberately conservative in method and ambitious only in
*question*. The goal is not to publish a new physics, but to enter foundational
physics through one lens and see where it earns its keep — under hard
constraints (Lorentz invariance, unitarity, no-signaling, thermodynamics, known
experimental bounds) and with an explicit standing rule **not** to inflate into
a grand theory of everything.

## The lens

🔴 candidate coordinate system. The conjecture the line explores: what is
fundamental may be the structure of **lawful state-update and information**, with
matter / energy / space / time read as stable projections of it, rather than
information being something that lives *on top of* a pre-given spacetime of
things. Held as a working frame, not a claim.

## Established anchors the line takes seriously

🟢 These are mainstream theoretical physics, used as the ground the lens has to
respect:

- **Bekenstein–Hawking entropy** `S = A / 4ℓ_P²` — black-hole entropy scales
  with *area*, not volume; the origin of the holographic principle (a region's
  maximum information content is bounded by its boundary). Suggests the bulk is
  not fundamental.
- **Page curve, resolved (≈2019–2020).** Hawking radiation was long thought
  thermal (information loss); entanglement-wedge / island computations realized
  the Page curve and settled the *direction* — information is **preserved** in
  the entanglement structure of the radiation, just maximally scrambled.
  (Direction is consensus; full mechanism is still active research.)
- **ER = EPR / entanglement-built geometry** — entangled pairs as
  Einstein–Rosen bridges; "building spacetime from entanglement"; sever the
  entanglement and the geometry disconnects.
- **Black holes as maximal scramblers / ultimate computers** — saturating the
  quantum-chaos bound on scrambling; the densest physical information processors
  allowed by known limits.
- **Tensor-network reconstructions** (MERA-style) — spacetime as a network of
  entanglement; a black hole as a region of maximally concentrated entanglement.

The honest summary: across AdS/CFT, tensor-network, and computational-substrate
readings, **black holes (and arguably spacetime) increasingly look like
phenomenological faces of more fundamental information structure, not
ontological primitives.** Caveats kept on the record: not universal (Penrose
dissents; de Sitter / our actual universe holography is incomplete), and the
strongest "interface" readings are minority positions used only as frames.

## A candidate structural isomorphism

🔴 speculative, confidence low, explicitly *not* a proven mapping.

A line worth stating precisely so it can be attacked: an information-density
bound in a finite region may be **one motif instantiated on two substrates.**

| Saturate an information-density ceiling in a finite region | Get a horizon |
| --- | --- |
| Bekenstein bound saturated | Event horizon (causal) — black-hole formation |
| "Cognitive cone" bound saturated (max rate an agent can compress/propagate information through itself) | Epistemic horizon — a predictive-capability ceiling |

If the isomorphism held rigorously, spacetime horizons would become instances of
the same density-saturation motif identified for cognition. It does **not** hold
rigorously yet; the claim is only that the *shape* is suggestive and the mapping
is worth trying to break.

## Discipline on this line

The standing rule is to resist cosmology-expansion. The bottleneck is
observables and falsifiers, not vocabulary. Concretely:

- Prefer **literature, constraints, toy models, and affordable open-data
  re-analysis** over manifesto.
- A reachability/transition rewrite of a physics puzzle is interesting only if
  it (a) respects unitarity / no-signaling / thermodynamics and (b) suggests a
  counterfactual that a "standard" reading does not.
- Anomaly/boundary evidence (e.g. sensor anomalies) is treated as a test of
  instruments and theories, never as evidence *for* a preferred story.

## Open questions (where collaboration helps most)

1. Where does a reachability/transition reading add predictive content beyond
   restating the holographic / Page-curve results?
2. Can the "cognitive-cone" bound be defined sharply enough that a
   Bekenstein-style derivation either goes through or visibly fails?
3. What is the minimal toy model in which "information changes future
   reachability" is *measured*, not asserted — and what would falsify it?
